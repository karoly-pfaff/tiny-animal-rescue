# Garden

## Garden ambience — Sunlit Helpers

- Runtime ID: `music.location.garden.ambient`
- Final edited loop: 60–120 seconds

### Suno style prompt

```text
Warm instrumental garden ambience for a premium preschool animal-rescue game. Nylon guitar, gentle kalimba, soft flute, light pizzicato strings, and tiny brushed shakers at about 78 BPM. Sunny, caring, curious, and fresh, with a simple major-key motif that leaves space for birds, narration, and interaction effects. Suggest flowers moving in a breeze and friendly animals nearby without imitating them musically. Even dynamics and a stable middle section suitable for looping. No vocals, ukulele cliché, nursery rhyme, comic bounce, busy melody, sharp bells, heavy beat, tension, sadness, or dramatic ending.
```

## Garden mission variation — Gentle Helping Hands

- Runtime ID: `music.location.garden.mission`
- Final edited loop: 45–90 seconds

### Suno style prompt

```text
Focused but calm instrumental problem-solving music for a preschool rescue mission in a sunny garden. Soft marimba pulse, muted nylon guitar, pizzicato strings, gentle flute fragments, and minimal brushed percussion around 80 BPM. Caring concentration with no urgency: the child is moving a ladder, finding a toy, or helping a small animal. Sparse melody, rounded transients, stable harmony, and room for spoken prompts and touch effects. Loop-friendly middle with no climax. No vocals, ticking, sneaking music, suspense, danger, slapstick, heavy drums, bright staccato overload, or triumphant ending.
```
