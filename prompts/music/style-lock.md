# Tiny Rescue music style lock

Compose gentle instrumental ambient music for a premium preschool animal-rescue game. Warm, organic, melodic but unobtrusive, emotionally safe, lightly playful, and comfortable under repeated listening. Use a small acoustic palette: felt piano, soft marimba or kalimba, pizzicato strings, nylon guitar, harp, gentle woodwinds, brushed hand percussion, and subtle airy pads. Simple consonant harmony, clear but sparse motifs, rounded transients, low dynamic contrast, no tension build. Leave space for narration and interaction effects. The track should feel caring and quietly adventurous rather than sleepy, silly, or educational.

## Shared musical identity

- instrumental only;
- major, pentatonic, suspended, or similarly gentle harmonic language;
- mostly 64–84 BPM depending on location;
- no dramatic tempo changes;
- no prominent bass or dense low mids;
- no lead melody that competes with spoken prompts;
- natural phrase boundaries suitable for extracting a seamless-feeling loop;
- calm ending is acceptable, but the usable middle must remain steady.
