# Tiny Rescue visual style lock

Use this block in every generated Tiny Rescue image unless an approved art-direction revision supersedes it.

```text
Style/medium:
Premium 3D children's storybook illustration for a warm preschool animal-rescue game. Rounded, tactile, handcrafted forms; soft detailed fur and feathers; painted wood, woven fabric, felt, and natural foliage; polished feature-animation quality without photorealism.

Character language:
Friendly expressive animals with large readable eyes, soft proportions, clear silhouettes, gentle smiles, and preschool-safe emotions. Cute but not babyish, exaggerated but anatomically coherent. No threatening pose or visible suffering.

Lighting/mood:
Warm natural daylight, soft global illumination, gentle contact shading, cheerful and reassuring atmosphere. Saturated but harmonious colors with enough value contrast to distinguish interactive objects.

Composition language:
Clear foreground, middle ground, and background; large readable focal shapes; uncluttered interaction zones; decorative detail concentrated away from required sources, targets, paths, and runtime UI safe areas.

Continuity:
Maintain consistent rendering, palette, material response, camera height, character proportions, eye style, fur detail, and rounded wooden UI language across the Tiny Rescue project.
```

## Reference palette direction

- leaf green and grass green for positive primary actions;
- warm honey/orange for rescue emphasis and secondary actions;
- clear sky blue for navigation and calm utility actions;
- cream/white outlines for readable presentation typography;
- natural wood browns for signs and framing;
- avoid dirty grey overlays, neon cyber colors, harsh black UI, or moody cinematic grading.

Color is never the sole interaction cue.
