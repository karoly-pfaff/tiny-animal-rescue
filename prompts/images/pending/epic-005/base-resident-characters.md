## Shared output contract

- Classification: production-safe candidate.
- Output: 1254×1254 PNG with genuine transparency and clean alpha edges.
- Composition: one complete full-body resident, centered with generous padding and a consistent
  approximate scale across the complete cast.
- Canonical state: neutral, friendly three-quarter standing or resting pose with every stable
  marking clearly visible.
- Shelter idle state: relaxed, welcoming pose suitable for indefinite display; no implied need,
  waiting task, hunger, illness, dirt, sadness, or obligation.
- Shelter happy state: a small, readable joyful reaction that can return cleanly to idle.
- Style: premium 3D children's storybook illustration; rounded tactile forms, soft detailed fur,
  feathers, scales, or skin; large readable eyes; gentle proportions; warm natural daylight; soft
  global illumination; harmonious saturated color; polished feature-animation quality without
  photorealism.
- Identity continuity: canonical, idle, and happy outputs for one resident must preserve markings,
  eye color, proportions, ear/tail/beak/shell shape, and every other stable feature.
- No environment, floor, water patch, ground patch, platform, pedestal, base, frame, border, cast
  shadow, unrelated prop, or accessory.
- No text, letters, numerals, pseudo-writing, UI, logo, signature, watermark, brand mark, generator
  tag, promotional mark, or automated-authorship credit.
- Reject cropped anatomy, malformed or duplicated limbs, duplicated residents, asymmetrical eyes,
  uncanny expressions, photorealistic suffering, threatening poses, dirt/injury detail, harsh
  shadows, excessive blur, or noisy alpha edges.
