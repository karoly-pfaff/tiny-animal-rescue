# Example: Indoor shelter production background

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Tiny Rescue shelter-area background
Classification: production candidate
Shelter area ID: indoor-room

Primary request:
Create a cozy indoor rescue shelter where four small rescued residents can rest,
play, and be tapped comfortably.

Environment:
Warm timber beams, cream plaster walls, a large open window toward a sunny garden,
soft woven rugs, baskets, cushions, a scratching post, toy storage, and gentle
houseplants. Materials feel clean, tactile, and well cared for.

Resident zones:
- left: round green bed beside the scratching post;
- center-left: soft blue bed with open floor around it;
- center-right: pink cushion and nearby food-bowl position;
- right: pale blue bed beside a small wooden animal house.

Style:
Apply the Tiny Rescue visual style lock.

Composition:
Landscape 4:3, 1024×768. Four distinct non-overlapping zones with enough room for
small idle animations. Keep the top-center title region, upper-left back control,
upper-right progress area, and bottom action region visually quiet.

Technical requirements:
Background only. Residents, bowls, interactive toys, labels, and UI are composited separately.

Constraints:
No animals, UI, text, signs containing glyphs, labels, counters, buttons, locked
silhouettes, watermark, or duplicated furniture. Apply global production-scene constraints.
```
