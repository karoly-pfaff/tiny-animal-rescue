# Example: Wooden ladder interactive prop

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Tiny Rescue interactive prop
Classification: production candidate
Prop ID: garden-kitten-tree-ladder

Subject:
One small child-friendly wooden garden ladder with five broad rungs, rounded rails,
smooth honey-colored wood, subtle painted-green metal fasteners, and no damage.

Interaction role:
Drag source that a preschool child moves toward a tree trunk.

View and shape:
Three-quarter front view, upright with a very slight backward lean. Strong clean
silhouette, wide rungs, visibly light and safe rather than tall or industrial.

Style:
Apply the Tiny Rescue visual style lock. Match the warm morning light, camera height,
and perspective of the `garden-kitten-tree` scene.

Composition:
Square high-resolution image. One centered ladder, completely visible, generous
transparent padding on every side, orientation suitable for placing against the tree.

Technical requirements:
Genuinely transparent background with clean alpha edges.

Constraints:
No tree, grass, floor, ground patch, wall, animal, person, hand, arrow, dashed target,
platform, pedestal, cast shadow, text, label, logo, watermark, or second object.
```
