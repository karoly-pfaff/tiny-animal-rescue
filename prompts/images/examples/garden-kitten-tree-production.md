# Example: Garden kitten mission production scene

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Tiny Rescue mission scene
Classification: production candidate
Mission ID: garden-kitten-tree

Primary request:
Create the clean illustrated scene for rescuing Mimi, a kitten waiting on a low,
wide tree branch while a wooden ladder can be dragged into place.

Scene/backdrop:
A warm garden beside the rescue center, with a large leafy tree, flowers, a low
wooden fence, a distant cottage, blue sky, and soft morning sunlight.

Characters:
Mimi, a small gray-and-white kitten, waits on a broad branch with a concerned but
safe expression. A cheerful brown-and-white rabbit helper stands on the grass and
looks toward the empty ladder position.

Interactive composition:
- source: one small wooden ladder, fully visible in the lower-right region;
- target: a broad clear zone on the lower tree trunk in the center-left;
- interaction corridor: open grass and air between ladder and trunk;
- success state: the ladder can appear securely against the trunk and Mimi can descend.

Style:
Apply the Tiny Rescue visual style lock.

Composition:
Landscape 4:3, 1024×768. Keep upper-left and lower-right safety margins available
for runtime controls without obscuring the ladder. Decorative flowers stay outside
the drag corridor.

Technical requirements:
Clean scene concept for later layer extraction. No runtime UI.

Constraints:
No title, instruction, button, arrow, dashed target, hand pointer, step tracker,
letters, numbers, sign text, logo, or watermark. Apply global production-scene constraints.
```
