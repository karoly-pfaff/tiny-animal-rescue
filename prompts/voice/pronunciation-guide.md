# Pronunciation approval guide

Pronunciation is approved per locale before batch production. Use a native Hungarian reviewer for HU
and a native English reviewer for EN; do not infer one locale's reading from the other.

## Approval record

For every resident and any ambiguous place or action word, record:

- the semantic ID and locale;
- an approved plain-language reading note or a short audition reference;
- the native reviewer's disposition and date;
- any stress, vowel-length, or name-specific correction;
- the batch and narrator to which the decision applies.

Do not place phonetic directions inside the exact spoken line. Do not imitate or clone a real person's
voice without documented rights and consent.

## First-rescue lock

The first-rescue audition must cover `Mimi`, `létra`, and `biztonságban` in Hungarian and `Mimi`,
`ladder`, and `safe` in English. Approve the natural reading in each locale before generating the
remaining lines. Mimi's name stays recognizably consistent across mission, success, and shelter lines.

Pronunciation references must be clean source material. They may not contain an audible watermark,
generator tag, spoken brand or service name, promotional tag, or sonic signature.
