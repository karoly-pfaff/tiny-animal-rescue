# Interaction primitive effects

Apply `style-lock.md` to every prompt.

## Tap/remove

### `effects.interaction.tap-remove`

```text
Create one tiny tactile removal sound for tapping a loose leaf, reed, twig, or soft obstacle: a rounded fingertip tap plus a quiet dry natural rustle, 0.20–0.40 seconds, mono. Provide three material-neutral variants suitable for rapid repetition. No snapping branch, crunch, impact, voice, musical note, or long ambience.
```

### `effects.interaction.obstacle-cleared`

```text
Create one gentle obstacle-cleared confirmation: two soft organic taps ending in a tiny warm upward sparkle, 0.45–0.75 seconds, mono. Encouraging but smaller than completing a whole step. No fanfare, coin, applause, sharp bell, speech, or long reverb.
```

## Drag-to-target

### `effects.interaction.drag-pick-up`

```text
Create one soft object-pick-up sound: a tiny felt-and-wood lift with a subtle upward motion, 0.18–0.32 seconds, mono, dry. The object should feel light, safe, and touchable. Provide two variants. No suction pop, game inventory sound, digital beep, voice, or sparkle.
```

### `effects.interaction.drag-move`

```text
Create one very quiet continuous drag texture, about 1.5 seconds, designed to loop or be volume-modulated while a wooden or fabric object follows a finger. Soft dry cloth over smooth wood, minimal high frequencies, mono, no bumps or rhythm. No scrape, squeak, friction harshness, melody, voice, or environment ambience.
```

### `effects.interaction.drag-snap`

```text
Create one correct-placement sound: soft wood settling into place followed by a small warm two-note organic sparkle, 0.45–0.75 seconds, mono, gently rising and satisfying. Provide three subtle variants. No magnet clang, coin, digital chime, glass ping, applause, voice, or long tail.
```

### `effects.interaction.drag-return`

```text
Create one gentle object-return sound after an incorrect drop: a quiet soft cloth glide and cushioned wooden settle, 0.45–0.75 seconds, mono, emotionally neutral. It must not sound like failure or punishment. No descending sad tone, buzzer, boing, whoosh blast, voice, or impact.
```

## Wipe/clean

### `effects.interaction.wipe-stroke`

```text
Create one soft cleaning-stroke sound: a small damp cloth brushing smoothly over fur-safe or painted surface, 0.35–0.70 seconds, mono, gentle and clean. Provide three variants with subtle texture differences for repeated strokes. No wet squelch, spraying, harsh rubbing, squeak, voice, rhythm, or room ambience.
```

### `effects.interaction.wipe-progress`

```text
Create one tiny clean-progress accent: a quiet airy glint with a soft felt-mallet touch, 0.20–0.35 seconds, mono, subtle enough to repeat at progress thresholds. No glass ping, coin, bright bell, musical phrase, voice, or long sparkle.
```

### `effects.interaction.wipe-complete`

```text
Create one warm cleaning-complete cue: a final soft cloth sweep followed by two rounded gentle sparkles, 0.70–1.10 seconds, mono. Fresh and satisfying without implying sterile shine. No spray bottle, bubble pop sequence, magic wand, applause, voice, or piercing high frequencies.
```

## Match

### `effects.interaction.match-pick-up`

```text
Create one tiny friendly matching-piece lift: soft felt tap with a quiet wooden rise, 0.18–0.30 seconds, mono, dry. Provide two subtle variants. No plastic click, inventory pickup, digital tone, speech, or sparkle.
```

### `effects.interaction.match-success`

```text
Create one correct-pair sound: two complementary soft wooden tones meeting in a warm consonant interval, 0.45–0.75 seconds, mono, clear but not melodic. Provide three variants with the same meaning. No wrong-answer contrast, coin, bell, voice, applause, or long reverb.
```

## Trace

### `effects.interaction.trace-move`

```text
Create one quiet flowing trace texture, about 1.5 seconds, suitable for looping while a finger follows a broad path: soft airy brush with tiny muted wooden droplets, smooth and non-rhythmic, restrained stereo width. No rushing wind, water splash, scraping, ticking, melody, voice, or bright particles.
```

### `effects.interaction.trace-progress`

```text
Create one subtle trace checkpoint cue: a tiny warm rounded droplet-like mallet sound, 0.18–0.30 seconds, mono, soft enough for several uses in one path. Provide three micro-variants. No sharp water drop, sonar ping, coin, digital beep, voice, or reverb.
```

### `effects.interaction.trace-complete`

```text
Create one calm path-complete cue: three very soft organic tones flowing upward into a warm settled note, 0.75–1.20 seconds, mono. Encouraging and fluid, smaller than the mission celebration. No arpeggio flourish, magic wand, fanfare, applause, voice, or long cinematic tail.
```
