# Tiny Rescue effects style lock

```text
Create one clean, polished audio effect for a premium preschool animal-rescue game.
Warm, soft, organic, tactile, friendly, and immediately understandable. Rounded
transients, controlled high frequencies, low sensory intensity, and no startling
volume jump. Use natural materials such as soft wood, felt, cloth, paper, water,
gentle hand percussion, tiny mallets, and subtle organic sparkles when appropriate.
Isolated source with minimal room tone, no music, no melody, no speech, no narration,
no crowd, no unrelated background event, no watermark, and no long cinematic reverb.
```

## Rejection versus success

- Success is brighter, slightly rising, and warm.
- Rejection is quieter, shorter, softer, and neutral—not descending in a sad or scolding way.
- Major progress may use two or three layered organic elements but never a loud impact.

## Repetition safety

Repeated effects should tolerate rapid playback without becoming shrill. Avoid narrow piercing frequencies, hard clicks, excessive stereo width, and long overlapping tails.
