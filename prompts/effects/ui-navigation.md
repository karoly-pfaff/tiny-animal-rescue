# UI and navigation effects

Apply `style-lock.md` to every prompt.

## `effects.ui.confirm`

```text
Create one very short friendly button-confirm sound: a soft rounded wooden tap followed by a tiny warm felt-mallet tone, gently rising, 0.25–0.40 seconds, mono, dry and clean. It should confirm an ordinary positive selection without sounding like a reward. Provide three subtle variants. No click, beep, speech, melody, coin sound, sparkle shower, or reverb tail.
```

## `effects.ui.back`

```text
Create one short calm back-navigation sound: a soft brushed wooden tick with a quiet airy reverse movement, neutral and reassuring, 0.25–0.45 seconds, mono, dry. The motion may suggest returning leftward but must not sound negative or descending sadly. No error tone, whoosh blast, click, beep, speech, or long tail.
```

## `effects.ui.home`

```text
Create one gentle home-navigation sound: a warm two-part wooden tap and soft low felt-mallet arrival, 0.40–0.65 seconds, mono, dry, safe and settled. It should feel like returning to a familiar place. No door slam, house ambience, melody, voice, heavy bass, or cinematic reverb.
```

## `effects.ui.location-select`

```text
Create one friendly map-location selection sound: a soft leaf-and-wood tap followed by a tiny rounded marimba bloom, 0.35–0.60 seconds, mono. Curious and inviting, slightly brighter than an ordinary button but not a reward. Provide two variants. No map paper rustle, speech, bell ping, fanfare, or long sparkle tail.
```

## `effects.ui.page-swipe`

```text
Create one gentle horizontal page-swipe sound for moving between shelter areas: soft cloth and paper-like movement with a tiny wooden settling tap, 0.45–0.75 seconds, stereo with restrained width, smooth and quiet. No harsh whoosh, wind gust, zipper, mechanical slider, voice, or musical tone.
```

## `effects.ui.prompt-replay`

```text
Create one small prompt-replay acknowledgement: a soft circular wooden double tap with a light breathy accent, 0.30–0.50 seconds, mono, neutral and clear. It must not compete with the narration that starts immediately after it. No speech, echo, rewind tape, digital beep, melody, or tail longer than half a second.
```

## `effects.ui.gentle-reject`

```text
Create one quiet neutral invalid-action cue for a preschool game: a soft felt-covered wooden wobble, two very low-intensity rounded notes with almost no pitch drop, 0.30–0.55 seconds, mono, dry. It should mean “try somewhere else” without sounding wrong, sad, or scolding. No buzzer, error beep, raspberry, boing comedy, voice, alarm, or sharp transient.
```

## `effects.ui.parent-gate-open`

```text
Create one restrained parent-area opening cue: a soft wooden latch movement and warm muted tone, 0.45–0.70 seconds, mono, clean and calm. Slightly more conventional than child controls but still part of the same tactile world. No lock click suggesting security failure, no digital beep, speech, jingle, or reverb.
```
