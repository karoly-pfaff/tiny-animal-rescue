# Resident animal reactions

Generate clean isolated reactions with no environment or music. Create two or three subtle variants when the reaction may repeat in the shelter.

## `effects.animal.mimi-kitten.greet`

```text
Create one tiny friendly kitten greeting: a soft short high meow followed by a quiet purr onset, affectionate and relaxed, 0.6–1.2 seconds, mono, close and clean. Provide three natural variants. No adult cat yowl, distress, hiss, human imitation, collar bell, room tone, or music.
```
