# Rescue and reward effects

Apply `style-lock.md` to every prompt.

## `effects.progress.step-complete`

```text
Create one reusable mission-step completion cue: soft wooden confirmation plus a tiny two-note warm mallet rise, 0.55–0.85 seconds, mono, gentle and clear under music. It should feel more important than placing one object but smaller than completing a mission. No coin, star rating, applause, voice, brass, sharp bell, or long reverb.
```

## `effects.progress.mission-complete`

```text
Create one warm mission-complete effect: layered soft marimba, harp glint, gentle leaf-like sweep, and a cushioned low wooden arrival, 1.5–2.5 seconds, stereo with restrained width. Joyful, caring, and proud without loudness or spectacle. Leave room for the spoken animal name. No vocals, crowd cheer, applause, cymbal crash, brass fanfare, coin shower, or explosive sparkle.
```

## `effects.progress.resident-unlock`

```text
Create one resident-unlock sound for a rescued animal joining the shelter: a soft fabric unfurl, warm wooden chime, and tiny affectionate sparkle, 1.2–2.0 seconds, stereo, welcoming and homelike. No padlock click, achievement jingle, loot reveal, rarity sound, applause, speech, or dramatic reverb.
```

## `effects.progress.shelter-arrival`

```text
Create one gentle shelter-arrival transition: a quiet leafy or cloth movement followed by a soft warm room-like settling tone, 0.8–1.4 seconds, stereo, safe and cozy. No door slam, transport whoosh, footsteps, voice, melody, or long ambience bed.
```

## `effects.progress.world-improved`

```text
Create one calm environmental-improvement cue for a cleaned pond or repaired area: a soft natural sweep, two rounded wooden tones, and a restrained airy brightness, 1.0–1.8 seconds, stereo. Positive and restorative, not magical or triumphant. No construction impact, trash noise, magic wand, fanfare, speech, or applause.
```

## `effects.progress.all-residents-safe`

```text
Create one special but gentle collection-complete effect: warm marimba, soft harp, pizzicato strings, and a quiet felt-piano arrival forming a short affectionate phrase, 2.5–4.0 seconds, stereo. Proud, tender, and complete without implying competition. No vocals, choir, huge orchestra, brass, cymbal crash, applause, fireworks, or mobile-game reward shower.
```
