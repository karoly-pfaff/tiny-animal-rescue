# Environmental ambience loops

These are effects-channel ambience beds, not music. Generate clean stereo source, then edit a seamless 20–60 second loop. Keep all events sparse so narration remains intelligible.

## `effects.ambience.rescue-center`

```text
Create a calm sunny rescue-center garden ambience: very soft breeze in leaves, distant small birds, occasional gentle wing flutter, faint garden movement, no close animal vocalization. Stereo, natural, low density, 45–60 seconds, seamless-loop source. No people, traffic, van engine, tools, door, music, speech, dog bark, rooster, loud bird call, wind gust, or identifiable repeating pattern.
```
